Effect probe

Measures the two things every scene manifest has to declare and nobody can eyeball reliably: coverage — how much of the frame the effect lights up, which the director uses to decide whether a boundary can be cut or has to be dissolved — and motion — what the picture actually does, which decides which effects can flow into which. Paste the numbers it prints into effect.json. See Flow.cs for what the motion classes mean.

Coverage is authoritative; motion is a screen. Coverage is measured directly and a disagreement over about 0.10 means the manifest is wrong. Motion is inferred by fitting a centred zoom and a translation to two frames a third of a second apart, and that model only sees head-on travel: a corridor flight with the camera off-axis or rolling, or a field of points too sparse for a warp to align, both come back as churn however plainly they are flights. So a declares motion flag is a prompt to go and look at the effect, not a verdict — several in this library are correct as declared and the probe is the thing that is wrong. BARELY MOVES is the flag that is always worth acting on.
